# Inorganic chemistry reference notes

Sulfuric acid is a dense, colorless mineral acid used in lead-acid batteries and fertilizer production.

$$
H2SO4
$$

The sulfate anion is a polyatomic ion carrying a double negative charge, formed by oxidation of sulfur dioxide.

$$
SO4^2-
$$

Calcium hydroxide, also known as slaked lime, is a sparingly soluble base produced by treating quicklime with water.

$$
Ca(OH)2
$$

Ammonium sulfate is an inorganic salt widely used as an agricultural fertilizer because it supplies both nitrogen and sulfur.

$$
(NH4)2SO4
$$

Cobalt is a hard, lustrous, ferromagnetic transition metal used in high-strength alloys and lithium-ion battery cathodes.

$$
Co
$$

Carbon monoxide is a colorless, odorless, toxic gas produced by incomplete combustion of carbon-containing fuels.

$$
CO
$$

Hydrated copper sulfate forms bright blue triclinic crystals that lose their water of crystallization on heating.

$$
CuSO4*5H2O
$$

The synthesis of water from its elements is the classic exothermic combination reaction of hydrogen and oxygen.

$$
\ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}
$$
