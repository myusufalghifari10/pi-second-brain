# Engineering Units Primer

## Torque

Torque couples force to a lever arm and is measured in newton metres, written N·m.
A 40 N·m wrench setting is typical for car wheel bolts, and mechanics often type
N m instead of using the middle dot.

## Energy

Electricity bills measure consumption in kilowatt hours, written kW·h. Running a
2 kW heater for three hours uses 6 kWh of energy, and distributors meter the
kilowatt hour rather than the joule.

## Viscosity

Dynamic viscosity is expressed in pascal seconds, Pa·s. Water flows near 0.001 Pa·s
at room temperature, while honey pours closer to 10 Pa s, and engine oils sit
somewhere in between.

## Resistivity

Electrical resistivity is quoted in ohm metres, Ω·m. Copper conducts with a
resistivity of 1.68e-8 Ω·m, silver is lower still, and good insulators reach
1e16 Ω m.

## Microgram doses

Pharmacology doses are often measured in micrograms, µg. A vitamin D tablet may
carry 25 µg of active compound, and laboratory scales resolve single µg masses.
