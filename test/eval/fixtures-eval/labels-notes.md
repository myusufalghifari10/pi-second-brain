# Electrostatics Notes

## Gauss Law

The integral form of Gauss's law relates the electric flux through a closed surface
to the total enclosed charge, as captured by the surface equation below.

$$\oint_S \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{A} = \frac{Q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}$$

\label{eq:gauss-law}

## Flux Conservation

Taking the divergence of both sides shows that the magnetic flux through any closed
surface vanishes, so the enclosed magnetic charge is always zero for solenoidal fields.

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$$

\label{eq:flux-conservation}

As noted in \ref{eq:gauss-law}, the surface integral form and the divergence statement
are equivalent descriptions of the same physical conservation principle.
